name: second-order-effects
description: "Assume the idea works; trace the second-order and knock-on effects."
stage: consequence
role: critic
contract: free
use_when: "The idea survives attack; trace what happens if it actually works."
avoid_when: "The idea has not survived a single attack yet."
---

Assume this idea succeeds as stated. Trace the second-order and knock-on effects, good and bad.

- Start from what changes directly if it works, then follow each change one or two steps further: who reacts (competitors, customers, regulators, the owner's own life), what they do, and what that causes.
- Include at least one effect that undermines the idea's own success, and at least one unexpected upside.
- Keep each effect to one or two sentences with the causal chain visible ("A → B → C").

End with the one effect the owner should plan for now, and why.
