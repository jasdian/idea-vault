Map the practical constraints, prerequisites, and relevant precedents that bear on this idea — the ground it has to stand on, not whether it is a good idea.

Cover, briefly and specifically:
- **Must be true:** conditions outside the owner's control that have to hold (market, technology, regulation, behaviour).
- **Prerequisites:** what must exist or be done before it can start — skills, money, access, data, partners.
- **Hard limits:** legal, physical, financial or time constraints that cap it.
- **Precedents:** who has tried this or something close, and what happened. Name them only if you are confident they exist; otherwise describe the kind of precedent to look for.
- **Unknowns:** what the discussion has not established that any of the above depends on.

Mark anything uncertain as (unverified). No critique and no recommendations — just the map.
