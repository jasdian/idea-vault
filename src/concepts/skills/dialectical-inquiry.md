Apply dialectical inquiry to the idea below. Do not list objections — build a rival.

1. **Assumptions** — the 3 to 5 load-bearing assumptions the idea rests on.
2. **Counter-plan** — negate the most important of those assumptions and build the strongest alternative plan that pursues the same underlying goal on the opposite assumptions. Make it a plan someone could genuinely believe in, not a strawman.
3. **Head to head** — for each assumption, which plan does the evidence currently favour, and what observation would settle it?
4. **Synthesis** — what the idea should keep, drop, or steal from the counter-plan.
