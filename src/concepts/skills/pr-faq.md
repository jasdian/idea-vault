Work backwards from launch day, Amazon PR/FAQ style, for the idea below.

1. **Press release** (under 200 words), dated launch day: a headline; who the customer is; their problem in their own words; the solution and why it beats what they do today; one customer quote. Be concrete enough that a reader could say "that's not me" — vague is failure.
2. **FAQ** — the 6 hardest questions a skeptical customer, investor, or engineer would ask, each with an honest answer. Where the honest answer is "we don't know yet", write that and name what would find out.
3. **What the press release exposed** — the claims you could not write concretely. These are the idea's weakest points.
