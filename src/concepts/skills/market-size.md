Size the opportunity for the idea below with a quick, explicit estimate — the reasoning matters more than the number.

- **Who pays:** the specific buyer or user segment, and what they pay for.
- **Bottom-up estimate:** number of buyers × price or value per buyer × frequency, each factor with its assumption stated. Give a low / likely / high range.
- **Reachable share:** what fraction the owner could realistically reach in 2–3 years, and why.
- **Sanity check:** compare against one known reference point (a comparable company, category or spend) if you know one; mark it (unverified) if unsure.
- **Swing factor:** the one assumption that moves the estimate most — the thing worth checking first.

Do not present a guess as data. If the idea is not a business (a personal project, research, a policy), size its reach or impact instead with the same structure.
