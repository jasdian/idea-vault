name: converge
description: "Judge what the earlier moves found, claim by claim, and commit to one position: pursue, kill, or pursue only if."
stage: converge
role: synthesizer
contract: free
use_when: "Steelman, attack and consequence moves have run and the transcript is a pile of findings with no position."
avoid_when: "No attack move has run yet — you would be converging on an untested idea."
---

Converge the discussion below into ONE position. Do not summarise it and do not add new objections — judge the findings the earlier moves already produced.

1. **Verdict** — the first line is `Pursue`, `Kill`, or `Pursue only if <condition>`, then one sentence of why. Commit; a position that pleases every finding is not a position.
2. **What decides it** — the single most consequential finding. A reader who stops here should still have the thing that matters most.
3. **Findings, judged** — merge findings that share a mechanism, even when different moves raised them. One line each: the claim, the move(s) that raised it, and a verdict — CONFIRMED (the discussion itself holds the evidence), UNCERTAIN (plausible, unsettled), or REFUTED (the discussion answered it — say how). Keep refuted findings listed with their reason; never drop one silently. A finding is a claim to test, not a vote to count: three moves repeating one worry is one finding. If you would confirm everything, look again.
4. **What holds up** — the parts of the idea that survived attack. Do not skip this.
5. **The trade-off taken** — what this position accepts giving up, and at least one rejected alternative (a rival plan, a pivot, or not doing it) with why it lost.
6. **Kill criteria** — 2 or 3 observable signals that would reverse the verdict, each with the cheapest way to check it.
7. **Still open** — the UNCERTAIN items the verdict hangs on, as questions. For any that must be settled before building, state the default you are assuming until someone does.

Keep findings (what is true) apart from prescriptions (what to do), so a weak recommendation cannot borrow the credibility of a strong finding. Use only what the idea, its memory and the discussion say; do not invent evidence, numbers or customers, and say so where something was never examined. Short and ranked beats complete.
