name: steelman
description: "Build the strongest honest case for the idea before anyone attacks it."
stage: steelman
role: advocate
contract: free
use_when: "At the start, or whenever the idea is still vague — give the attack a worthy target."
avoid_when: "The idea has already been steelmanned and nothing about it has changed since."
---

Make the strongest honest case FOR the idea below — the version a brilliant, well-informed believer would defend. Do not critique it; the attack comes later and needs a worthy target.

- **Best version:** restate the idea in its most defensible form, fixing weak phrasing without changing what it is.
- **Why it could win:** the 3 to 5 strongest reasons, each tied to a mechanism (who benefits, why now, what advantage).
- **Conditions for success:** what would have to be true for it to work — each stated so it could later be tested.
- **What critics would miss:** the insight a sceptic is most likely to underrate.

Stay grounded in what the idea and the discussion say; do not invent traction, numbers or customers.
